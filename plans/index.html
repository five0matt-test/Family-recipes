Thursday · Nov 12
Smoky Seminole Pumpkin–White Bean Soup with Seared Chicken
Cranberry, Goat Cheese and Rosemary Sourdough Focaccia

Make ahead

Wednesday: mix the sourdough focaccia with ripe starter, finish folds and bulk fermentation, then refrigerate overnight. Thursday: allow 3–5 hours or longer for pan proofing, then top with cranberry sauce, goat cheese and rosemary, bake and cool before practice. The soup is dairy-free; the bread contains goat cheese. Wednesday: roast whole Seminole pumpkins while making dinner, then remove seeds, scoop and measure 6 cups flesh. Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers. Thursday: simmer and blend the soup on the stove, then transfer hot to the crockpot for practice. Blend all the white beans with the pumpkin until smooth. Grill or sear 2½ lb smoky lemon-garlic chicken thighs, chop and stir into the blended soup near the end. Serve your prepared Cherry Bomb hot sauce on the side. White beans provide creaminess without dairy or coconut milk. Keep at 140°F or above; reheat chilled soup on the stove to 165°F before transferring.
