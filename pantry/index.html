About your pantry

Only the two Google accounts listed in your Firestore security rules can read or change inventory. A recipe marked as cooked does not yet deduct ingredients; that is planned for Phase 3.

CSV and JSON exports let you share a current pantry snapshot for meal planning. Barcode scanning and voice input can be added after the shared inventory is tested.
