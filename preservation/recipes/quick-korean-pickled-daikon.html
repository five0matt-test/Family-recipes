Quick Korean-Style Pickled Daikon

Korean · Refrigerator pickling · Approx. 20 minutes

Storage: Refrigerated. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb daikon radish, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
  • ½ cup rice vinegar
  • ½ cup water
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • Optional: thinly sliced fresh ginger or a pinch of gochugaru

Directions

  1. Place daikon cubes in a clean lidded refrigerator-safe container.
  2. Warm vinegar, water, sugar and salt just enough to dissolve; do not reduce the liquid.
  3. Pour over the radish, add optional ginger or chili, and stir.
  4. Cool briefly and refrigerate promptly. Chill at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, before serving.
  5. Keep refrigerated, use clean utensils, and prepare small batches for best texture.

Notes & serving ideas

Excellent with bulgogi bowls and Korean barbecue. Keep refrigerated; this is a quick pickle, not fermented kimchi or a canning recipe. Best used within about 1 week.
