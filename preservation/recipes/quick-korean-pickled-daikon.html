Quick Korean-Style Pickled Daikon

Korean · Refrigerator pickling · Approx. 20 minutes

Storage: Refrigerated. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb daikon radish, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
  • ½ cup distilled white vinegar (5% acidity)
  • ½ cup water
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • Optional: thinly sliced fresh ginger or a pinch of gochugaru

Directions

  1. Wash the radish under running water before peeling and cubing. Place cubes in a clean lidded heatproof refrigerator-safe container.
  2. Warm vinegar, water, sugar and salt just enough to dissolve; do not reduce the liquid.
  3. Pour over the radish, add optional ginger or chili, and stir.
  4. Cool briefly and refrigerate promptly. Chill at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, before serving.
  5. Keep refrigerated, use clean utensils, and prepare small batches for best texture.

Notes & serving ideas

Excellent with bulgogi bowls and Korean barbecue. Keep refrigerated; this is a quick pickle, not fermented kimchi or a canning recipe. Use within 3 days as a conservative household limit, not a tested shelf life.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: These instructions have been reviewed against general preservation guidance, but the exact formula and shelf life have not been laboratory validated.

Keep at 40°F (4°C) or colder and make small batches. Use vinegar labeled 5% acidity; do not dilute the brine further or substitute citrus juice. Keep vegetables covered with brine; if more liquid is needed, make fresh brine at the same ratio. Do not can this recipe.

Wash hands, produce and equipment before preparation. Refrigerate perishable prepared food promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Use shallow containers for hot mixtures; do not wait for them to cool fully on the counter.
