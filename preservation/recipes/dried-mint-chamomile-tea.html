Dried Mint and Chamomile Tea Blend

Tea · Dehydrating · About 15 min active prep; total estimate 6 hr 15 min including drying. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable dried harvest; 2 tsp dried blend per cup

Storage: Cool completely and store airtight in a cool, dry, dark place. Check that all material is fully dry before packing; discard moldy food. Drying time depends on humidity, load and equipment.

Ingredients

  • 1 loosely packed cup fresh culinary mint leaves
  • 1 loosely packed cup correctly identified food-grade chamomile flower heads

Directions

  1. Use plants grown for food without unsuitable pesticide treatments, never florist flowers. Discard damaged material; gently rinse and dry the surface.
  2. Keep mint and chamomile separate in a single layer on dehydrator trays. Follow herb/flower directions for the machine, using 95–115°F as the herb range. Begin checking mint after 1 hour; dense flower centers can need longer than the leaves.
  3. Continue until mint crumbles and the entire flower head, including its center, is dry and brittle. Do not stop because petals alone feel dry. Cool completely before packing each plant separately airtight.
  4. For one cup, combine 1 tsp dried mint and 1 tsp dried chamomile. Pour over 8 fl oz freshly boiled water, cover and steep 5 minutes; strain. Blend only small amounts for immediate use.

Notes & serving ideas

Drying time is variable; 6 hours is an estimate, not a guaranteed endpoint. Store dry herbs in a cool, dark cupboard; discard moldy material. Chamomile may cause reactions in people sensitive to ragweed-related plants. This is a culinary beverage, not a medical treatment.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: Family Recipes culinary preparation informed by the references below; the exact formula and storage life have not been laboratory validated or kitchen-tested here. References do not validate it for canning.

Cool completely and store airtight in a cool, dry, dark place. Check that all material is fully dry before packing; discard moldy food. Drying time depends on humidity, load and equipment.

Work with clean hands, sound produce and clean equipment. Refrigerate prepared perishables promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C); cool hot mixtures in shallow small containers. Never thaw perishables on the counter.
