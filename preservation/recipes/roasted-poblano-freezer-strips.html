Roasted Poblano Freezer Strips

Mexican · Freezing · Approx. 40 minutes

Storage: Frozen. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb poblano peppers (or other sweet peppers)
  • Optional: ½ tsp kosher salt per pound, added after thawing

Directions

  1. Wash and thoroughly dry peppers. Place on a foil-lined sheet pan.
  2. Broil or roast at high heat, turning until skins blister on all sides.
  3. Place peppers in a covered heatproof bowl for 10 minutes to loosen skins.
  4. When cool enough to handle, peel away loose skins, remove stems and seeds, and cut into strips.
  5. Cool promptly, spread strips on a lined tray and freeze until firm; transfer to freezer bags, press out air and label.

Notes & serving ideas

Use in fajitas, quesadillas, soups and rice bowls. Add salt when cooking. Frozen roasted peppers soften on thawing, making them best for cooked dishes.
