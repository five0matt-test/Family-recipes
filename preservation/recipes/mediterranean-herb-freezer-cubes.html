Mediterranean Herb Freezer Cubes

Mediterranean · Freezing · Approx. 20 minutes

Storage: Frozen. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup washed, thoroughly dried mixed herb leaves (parsley, oregano, thyme and rosemary work well)
  • ¼–½ cup water, as needed
  • Optional: 1 tsp finely grated Meyer lemon zest

Directions

  1. Remove tough woody stems, then finely chop the herbs.
  2. Divide herbs among the wells of a clean ice-cube or silicone freezer tray.
  3. Add a little water to cover herbs and optionally sprinkle in lemon zest.
  4. Freeze promptly until solid, then transfer cubes to a labeled freezer bag.
  5. Add frozen cubes directly to cooked sauces, braises, soups or marinades.

Notes & serving ideas

Use for lemon-herb chicken, Mediterranean beef, roasted vegetables and rice pilaf. Water-based cubes avoid room-temperature herb-in-oil storage concerns. Best flavor within 2–3 months.
