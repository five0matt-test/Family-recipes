Blanched Freezer Greens for Soups

Southern · Approx. 45 minutes

Storage: Frozen. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh collard greens or kale
  • Large pot of boiling water
  • Large bowl of ice water

Directions

  1. Wash greens thoroughly, remove tough stems and chop leaves to soup-ready size.
  2. Use a large pot of vigorously boiling water. Water-blanch collards for 3 minutes or kale for 2 minutes, starting timing when the water returns to a boil. Use small batches so boiling resumes within 1 minute.
  3. Immediately cool in ice water for the same time used for blanching, then drain thoroughly.
  4. Package cooled greens in freezer-safe containers with ½-inch headspace, or freezer bags with excess air removed. Label and freeze promptly.
  5. Add frozen greens to caldo verde, Southern-style greens, or soups during cooking.

Notes & serving ideas

This guide covers collards and kale only. Follow the linked NCHFP greens and blanching procedures; other vegetables require their own guidance.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: These instructions have been reviewed against general preservation guidance, but the exact formula and shelf life have not been laboratory validated.

Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or colder. Thaw at 40°F (4°C) or below in the refrigerator, or add frozen portions directly while cooking. Never thaw on the counter. Frozen storage times stated here describe quality, not a validated shelf life.

Wash hands, produce and equipment before preparation. Refrigerate perishable prepared food promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Use shallow containers for hot mixtures; do not wait for them to cool fully on the counter.
