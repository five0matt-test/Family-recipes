Blanched Freezer Greens for Soups

Southern · Approx. 45 minutes

Storage: Frozen. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh collard greens or kale
  • Large pot of boiling water
  • Large bowl of ice water

Directions

  1. Wash greens thoroughly, remove tough stems and chop leaves to soup-ready size.
  2. Consult the linked NCHFP vegetable-specific blanching guidance for the selected green and use its prescribed blanching time. Do not substitute a generic time.
  3. Immediately chill in ice water for the recommended cooling interval, then drain thoroughly.
  4. Portion cooled greens into labeled freezer bags, press out air, and freeze promptly.
  5. Add frozen greens to caldo verde, Southern-style greens, or soups during cooking.

Notes & serving ideas

Blanching varies by green. Check the authoritative crop-specific instructions before proceeding; this is a prep guide, not a substitute for those instructions.
