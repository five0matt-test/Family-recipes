Frozen Pumpkin or Winter Squash Purée

Versatile · Freezing · About 15 min active prep; total estimate 5 hr 10 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable; measure after cooking

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mature pumpkin or butternut squash, weighed before trimming

Directions

  1. Wash the outside. Cut into manageable sections and remove seeds. Mature Seminole pumpkin is used here only for freezing, not as a validated canning substitution.
  2. Steam the sections or bake at 350°F until flesh is soft throughout. Begin checking at 40 minutes; thick sections may take substantially longer.
  3. Scoop flesh from the rind and mash or blend without adding dairy. Place the pan in cold or ice water and stir to cool promptly.
  4. Divide into small freezer containers with ½-inch headspace. Label with measured cup volume and date, seal and freeze.
  5. Thaw in the refrigerator for baking or add frozen portions to simmering soups. Never home-can this purée.

Notes & serving ideas

The 70-minute estimate is preparation/cooking only and depends on squash thickness. Raw weight does not predict finished purée volume; weigh or measure after cooking. Freeze promptly after cooling.

Harvest reference batch: 2 lb whole pumpkin. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: Family Recipes culinary preparation informed by the references below; the exact formula and storage life have not been laboratory validated or kitchen-tested here. References do not validate it for canning.

Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Work with clean hands, sound produce and clean equipment. Refrigerate prepared perishables promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C); cool hot mixtures in shallow small containers. Never thaw perishables on the counter.
