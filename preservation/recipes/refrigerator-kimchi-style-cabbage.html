Fresh Korean-Style Cabbage (Refrigerated)

Korean · Refrigerator pickling · About 20 min active prep; total estimate 50 min including chilling. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 5 cups

Storage: Keep at 40°F (4°C) or below. Use within 3 days as a conservative household limit, not a tested shelf life. This recipe is not shelf-stable; do not can it or leave it out to ferment.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Napa cabbage, cored and cut into bite-size pieces
  • 4 oz daikon radish, peeled and cut into thin matchsticks
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru, plus more at serving if desired
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce for a vegetarian version)
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar labeled 5% acidity

Directions

  1. Rinse cabbage, daikon and green onions under running water; drain well. Use a clean bowl and utensils.
  2. Mix garlic, ginger, gochugaru, fish sauce, sugar, salt and vinegar. Toss thoroughly with the vegetables.
  3. Pack loosely into a clean covered container and refrigerate immediately at 40°F or below. Do not leave out to ferment.
  4. Chill 30 minutes, then taste and adjust heat at serving. Serve small portions with rice bowls; return the main container to the refrigerator promptly.

Notes & serving ideas

A fresh geotjeori-inspired side, not fermented kimchi or a long-storage preserve. Vinegar here is seasoning, not proof of a safe acidification process. Eat within 3 days as a conservative household practice; no validated storage life is claimed.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb Napa cabbage. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
