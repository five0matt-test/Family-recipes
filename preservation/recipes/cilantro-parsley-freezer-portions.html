Cilantro and Parsley Freezer Portions

Versatile · Freezing · About 15 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 15 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable; label each portion by measured spoonful

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz fresh cilantro, flat-leaf parsley or garlic chives
  • Water, only if using the optional cube method

Directions

  1. Pick out damaged leaves. Rinse herbs, drain and dry thoroughly. Use cilantro and parsley separately if you want distinct flavors.
  2. For the plain pack, chop the herbs. Spread a thin layer on a lined freezer tray and freeze until firm. Break into portions and immediately seal in a labeled freezer bag with excess air removed.
  3. Alternatively, loosely pack chopped herbs into clean small ice-cube wells and add just enough water to cover. Freeze solid, then transfer cubes to a labeled freezer bag.
  4. Keep frozen and add directly to hot soup, beans or rice. The herbs soften after thawing and are not a fresh-garnish substitute.

Notes & serving ideas

Garlic chives can be used as a separate culinary herb portion. The water-cube variation is an original convenience method; the reference supports freezing fresh herbs generally. Freeze promptly after preparation.

Harvest reference batch: 2 oz cilantro. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
