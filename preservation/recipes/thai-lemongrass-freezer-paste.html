Thai Lemongrass Freezer Paste

Thai · Freezing · Approx. 25 minutes

Storage: Frozen. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 6 lemongrass stalks, tender lower 4–6 inches only
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped ginger
  • ½ cup loosely packed Thai basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil or water, as needed for blending

Directions

  1. Trim tough lemongrass tops and root ends; peel away dry outer layers. Thinly slice the tender cores crosswise.
  2. Pulse lemongrass, garlic, ginger and Thai basil in a food processor until very finely minced.
  3. Add only enough water or oil to help the mixture blend into a coarse paste.
  4. Immediately divide into tablespoon-size portions in a silicone tray or small freezer containers.
  5. Freeze promptly, then transfer solid portions to a labeled freezer bag. Add directly to curries, stir-fries or marinades while cooking.

Notes & serving ideas

A fast starter for panang curry, Thai basil chicken and coconut soups. Keep frozen; never store garlic-in-oil paste at room temperature. Use within 2–3 months for best flavor.
