Garlic and Herb Freezer Portions (No Oil)

Mediterranean · Freezing · About 20 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 20 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable; label measured tablespoon portions

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz peeled garlic cloves
  • ½ cup chopped flat-leaf parsley or garlic chives
  • ¼ cup cold water, plus a little more only if needed to blend

Directions

  1. Rinse herbs and dry; peel garlic and trim damaged areas. Use clean equipment.
  2. Pulse garlic, herbs and water to a coarse paste. Use no oil in this recipe.
  3. Immediately divide into measured 1-tbsp portions in a clean small silicone tray. Freeze solid, then promptly transfer portions to a labeled freezer bag.
  4. Keep frozen. Add a portion directly to a hot pan, soup or sauce and cook through. Do not thaw portions on the counter or store the paste at room temperature.

Notes & serving ideas

Original water-based culinary blend; the garlic reference discusses garlic-in-oil hazards, not validation of this exact paste. A tablespoon is a convenience portion, not a guaranteed garlic-clove equivalent. Freeze immediately after mixing.

Harvest reference batch: 4 oz peeled garlic. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.

Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Work with clean hands, sound produce and clean equipment. Refrigerate prepared perishables promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C); cool hot mixtures in shallow small containers. Never thaw perishables on the counter.
