Thai Basil Freezer Portions

Thai · Approx. 15 minutes

Storage: Frozen. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 packed cups fresh Thai basil leaves
  • Cold water for rinsing
  • Optional: 2–4 tbsp water for chopped portions

Directions

  1. Pick healthy basil leaves and discard tough stems. Rinse and dry very thoroughly.
  2. For whole leaves, spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined tray and freeze until firm.
  3. Transfer frozen leaves to a labeled airtight freezer bag and return immediately to freezer.
  4. Alternatively, finely chop dried leaves, pack into small silicone molds with a splash of water, and freeze.
  5. Add frozen basil directly to hot stir-fries, soups or curries near the end of cooking. Do not expect thawed leaves to retain fresh salad texture.

Notes & serving ideas

Convenient for Thai basil chicken, panang beef and coconut soups. Freeze in small portions for cooking; best flavor within 2–3 months.
