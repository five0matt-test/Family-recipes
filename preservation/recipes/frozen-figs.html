Unsweetened Frozen Figs

Mediterranean · Freezing · About 25 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 25 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 1 lb before trimming

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fully ripe, sound figs

Directions

  1. Rinse figs gently, drain and remove stems. Peel if desired; leave whole or halve. Discard moldy or damaged fruit.
  2. Spread in one layer on a lined freezer tray. Freeze until firm.
  3. Transfer promptly into labeled freezer containers or bags, removing excess air; seal and keep frozen.
  4. Thaw in the refrigerator for compote or sauce, or cook from frozen. Expect a softer texture than fresh figs.

Notes & serving ideas

This is an unsweetened tray pack. Pale varieties may darken; the source provides an optional ascorbic-acid treatment if color retention matters. No extra fruit acid is needed for this freezer preparation.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb figs. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
