Smoky Salsa Tatemada — Freezer Batch

Mexican · Approx. 50 minutes

Storage: Frozen. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Roma or slicing tomatoes, halved
  • 2 medium jalapeños or serranos, stemmed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, to taste
  • 1 tbsp lime juice, to taste
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro, optional

Directions

  1. Heat broiler and line a rimmed pan with foil. Arrange tomatoes, chilies and onion cut-side up, with garlic tucked between.
  2. Broil until vegetables blister and char in spots, turning as needed, 10–18 minutes. Remove garlic earlier if browned.
  3. Let cool until safe to handle; peel garlic and discard tough chili stems.
  4. Pulse roasted vegetables and their juices to a chunky salsa. Stir in salt, lime juice and cilantro to taste.
  5. Cool promptly in shallow containers. Portion into freezer-safe containers, leaving expansion room; label and freeze. Thaw in the refrigerator.

Notes & serving ideas

A smoky taco salsa for carne asada, pollo adobado and grilled meats. Use more serrano for Dad or Son and less for Mom. This is not a tested canning recipe.
