Dried Mediterranean Herb Seasoning

Mediterranean · Dehydrating · About 30 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 30 min including drying. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable dried harvest; blend makes about 3 tbsp plus 2 tsp

Storage: Cool completely and store airtight in a cool, dry, dark place. Check that all material is fully dry before packing; discard moldy food. Drying time depends on humidity, load and equipment.

Ingredients

  • 1 loosely packed cup fresh oregano sprigs
  • 1 loosely packed cup fresh thyme sprigs
  • ½ loosely packed cup fresh rosemary sprigs
  • ½ loosely packed cup fresh sage leaves

Directions

  1. Discard damaged leaves. Rinse herbs and pat dry. Keep each herb separate so you can remove it when dry.
  2. Arrange a single loose layer on dehydrator trays. Dry at 95–115°F; humid conditions may require up to 125°F. Follow the dehydrator instructions and begin checking after 1 hour.
  3. Continue until leaves crumble readily and stems snap. A 1–4 hour range is only an estimate; do not package pliable or damp herbs.
  4. Cool completely, strip leaves and discard woody stems. Store each herb airtight in a cool, dark, dry cupboard.
  5. For a small blend, combine 2 tbsp dried oregano, 1 tbsp dried thyme, 1 tsp crushed dried rosemary and 1 tsp dried sage. If the harvest yields less, make a smaller blend in the same culinary ratio.

Notes & serving ideas

Drying yield varies widely. Keep leftover dried herbs separate. Crush just before use; start with 1 tsp blend per pound of vegetables or meat. Discard moldy herbs. If condensation appears after packing, remove herbs and finish drying; discard anything spoiled.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: Family Recipes culinary preparation informed by the references below; the exact formula and storage life have not been laboratory validated or kitchen-tested here. References do not validate it for canning.

Cool completely and store airtight in a cool, dry, dark place. Check that all material is fully dry before packing; discard moldy food. Drying time depends on humidity, load and equipment.

Work with clean hands, sound produce and clean equipment. Refrigerate prepared perishables promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C); cool hot mixtures in shallow small containers. Never thaw perishables on the counter.
