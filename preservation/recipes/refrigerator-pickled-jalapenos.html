Refrigerator Pickled Jalapeños

Mexican · Refrigerator pickling · About 25 min active prep; total estimate 24 hr 25 min including chilling. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 2–3 pint containers; yield varies

Storage: Keep at 40°F (4°C) or below. Use within 3 days as a conservative household limit, not a tested shelf life. This recipe is not shelf-stable; do not can it or leave it out to ferment.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb jalapeños, washed and sliced into ¼-inch rings
  • 1½ cups distilled white vinegar labeled 5% acidity
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 tbsp canning or pickling salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves, peeled and halved
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano

Directions

  1. Wear gloves to trim and slice the peppers. Wash hands, utensils and two heat-safe pint containers; check that the containers are intended for hot liquids.
  2. In a saucepan, bring vinegar, water, salt, sugar, garlic and oregano to a boil, stirring to dissolve. Add pepper rings and simmer 3 minutes.
  3. Ladle peppers and brine into the containers. Keep peppers covered with brine; divide between additional containers if needed rather than overpacking or adding plain water.
  4. Refrigerate promptly in small containers; do not leave on the counter until completely cold. Once chilled, cover securely. Chill 24 hours for flavor, then serve with clean utensils.

Notes & serving ideas

An original taco topping, not a canning formula. Banana peppers or pepperoncini may replace jalapeños for a milder refrigerated version; serranos are hotter. Do not reduce vinegar strength. This three-day household limit is conservative, not a validated pickle shelf life.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb jalapeños. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
