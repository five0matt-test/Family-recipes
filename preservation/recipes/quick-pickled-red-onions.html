Quick Refrigerator Pickled Red Onions

Mexican · Approx. 20 minutes

Storage: Refrigerated. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 large red onion (about 8 oz), thinly sliced
  • ½ cup distilled white vinegar, 5% acidity
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • Optional: ½ tsp Mexican oregano or 2 black peppercorns

Directions

  1. Place thinly sliced onion in a clean heatproof jar or lidded container.
  2. Bring vinegar, water, sugar and salt just to a simmer, stirring to dissolve. Add optional seasoning.
  3. Pour hot liquid over onions, pressing them beneath the liquid. Let stand briefly until steam subsides.
  4. Cover and refrigerate promptly. Chill at least 2 hours before serving.
  5. Keep refrigerated, use clean utensils, and make small batches for best quality; use within 3 days as a conservative household limit, not a tested shelf life.

Notes & serving ideas

For tacos, bowls, picanha and sandwiches. No cucumbers. This is a refrigerator pickle, not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: These instructions have been reviewed against general preservation guidance, but the exact formula and shelf life have not been laboratory validated.

Keep at 40°F (4°C) or colder and make small batches. Use vinegar labeled 5% acidity; do not dilute the brine further or substitute citrus juice. Keep vegetables covered with brine; if more liquid is needed, make fresh brine at the same ratio. Do not can this recipe.

Wash hands, produce and equipment before preparation. Refrigerate perishable prepared food promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Use shallow containers for hot mixtures; do not wait for them to cool fully on the counter.
