Mexican Refrigerator Escabeche

Mexican · Refrigerator pickling · Approx. 40 minutes

Storage: Refrigerated. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 6 jalapeños, sliced into thick rings
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and sliced thin
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup small cauliflower florets
  • 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 1 cup distilled white vinegar (5% acidity)
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 2 bay leaves
  • ½ tsp black peppercorns

Directions

  1. Wash vegetables and prepare a clean heatproof container with a lid.
  2. Combine vinegar, water, salt, sugar, oregano, bay leaves and peppercorns in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer.
  3. Add carrots and cauliflower and simmer 3 minutes. Add peppers, onion and garlic and simmer 1–2 minutes more; vegetables should remain crisp.
  4. Transfer vegetables to the container, cover with hot brine and let cool briefly.
  5. Refrigerate promptly. Allow at least 24 hours for flavor to develop; keep refrigerated and use clean utensils.

Notes & serving ideas

A crunchy topping for tacos, grilled beef and sandwiches. This is NOT shelf-stable and must not be water-bath canned. For best quality, make small batches and use within about 1 week.
