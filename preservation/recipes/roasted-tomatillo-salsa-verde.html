Roasted Tomatillo Salsa Verde — Freezer Batch

Mexican · Freezing · Approx. 45 minutes

Storage: Frozen. This is an original refrigerator/freezer recipe, NOT a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 1–2 jalapeños or serranos, stemmed (to taste)
  • 1 medium white or yellow onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • ½ cup chopped cilantro
  • 1–2 tbsp fresh lime juice, to taste
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste

Directions

  1. Heat broiler. Arrange tomatillos, chilies, onion and garlic on a foil-lined rimmed pan.
  2. Broil, turning as needed, until vegetables blister and soften, about 8–15 minutes. Watch garlic so it does not burn.
  3. Cool slightly, then pulse vegetables and their juices in a blender to your preferred texture.
  4. Stir in cilantro, lime juice and salt. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  5. Cool promptly in shallow containers. Portion into freezer-safe containers, leave ½-inch headspace, label and freeze. Thaw in the refrigerator.

Notes & serving ideas

Great with chicken tinga, carne asada and enchiladas. This is a freezer salsa, NOT a shelf-stable canning formula. Add more chili after thawing for hotter servings.

Review & safe storage

Original recipe: These instructions have been reviewed against general preservation guidance, but the exact formula and shelf life have not been laboratory validated.

Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or colder. Thaw at 40°F (4°C) or below in the refrigerator, or add frozen portions directly while cooking. Never thaw on the counter. Frozen storage times stated here describe quality, not a validated shelf life.

Wash hands, produce and equipment before preparation. Refrigerate perishable prepared food promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Use shallow containers for hot mixtures; do not wait for them to cool fully on the counter.
