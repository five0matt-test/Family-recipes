Frozen Roasted Tomato Sauce Starter

Italian · Freezing · About 20 min active prep; total estimate 5 hr 5 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable; measure after roasting

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ripe tomatoes, washed and cored
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, optional

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Halve small tomatoes or cut large ones into similar-size wedges. Place with garlic on a rimmed roasting tray.
  2. Toss with oil, oregano and optional salt. Roast 35–45 minutes until tomatoes collapse and edges brown; avoid burning the garlic.
  3. Mash or blend with the collected juices. Divide into shallow small containers and cool promptly; use an ice-water bath if helpful.
  4. Pack with expansion room according to the freezer-container directions, seal, label and freeze promptly.
  5. Thaw in the refrigerator or add frozen to a saucepan. Simmer and finish seasoning when making pasta sauce, beans or soup.

Notes & serving ideas

An original cooked freezer base. Yield depends on tomato water content. Added garlic and oil mean this must not be treated as a tested plain-tomato canning recipe; no acidity or process has been validated. The source supports freezing cooked tomato products generally.

Harvest reference batch: 2 lb tomatoes. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
