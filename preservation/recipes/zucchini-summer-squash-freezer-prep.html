Zucchini and Summer Squash Freezer Prep

Versatile · Freezing · About 30 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 30 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: Variable; measure grated packs for your baking recipe

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb young tender zucchini (or pattypan squash for the sliced method)
  • Ice and cold water, for cooling

Directions

  1. Wash and trim the squash. Choose one method: grated zucchini for baking, or sliced zucchini/pattypan for cooked dishes. Do not apply the grated method to pattypan.
  2. Grated zucchini: grate, then steam-blanch small batches for 1–2 minutes until translucent. Pack in measured recipe portions with ½-inch headspace. Place containers in cold water to cool promptly before sealing and freezing.
  3. Sliced squash: cut into ½-inch slices. Water-blanch for 3 minutes after boiling resumes, cool promptly in ice water and drain. Package with ½-inch headspace, seal, label and freeze.
  4. Label whether the pack is grated or sliced. Thaw grated packs in the refrigerator and drain excess liquid before baking; add sliced packs directly to cooked dishes.

Notes & serving ideas

Freezing softens squash. The zucchini batch reference is raw weight; measured grated volume varies. The source distinguishes steam-blanched grated zucchini from water-blanched sliced summer squash.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb zucchini. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
