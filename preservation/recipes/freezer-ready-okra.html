Freezer-Ready Okra

Cajun · Freezing · About 35 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 35 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 1 lb prepared okra before trimming loss

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh tender okra pods
  • Ice and cold water, for cooling

Directions

  1. Wash okra. Sort pods 4 inches or shorter separately from larger ones. Trim stem ends without opening the seed chambers.
  2. Water-blanch small pods for 3 minutes or large pods for 4 minutes, counting after the water returns to a boil. Work in small batches.
  3. Immediately cool in ice water, drain thoroughly, and leave whole or cut crosswise for gumbo.
  4. Pack in freezer containers with ½-inch headspace, seal, label and freeze promptly. Add frozen okra directly to a simmering dish and cook until tender.

Notes & serving ideas

This is plain frozen okra, not a canning recipe. Avoid tough, overgrown pods. The reference also offers a separate breaded method; this page does not include that variation.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb okra. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.

Review & safe storage

Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Work with clean hands, sound produce and clean equipment. Refrigerate prepared perishables promptly, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C); cool hot mixtures in shallow small containers. Never thaw perishables on the counter.
