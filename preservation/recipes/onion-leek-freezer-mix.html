Chopped Onion and Leek Freezer Mix

Weeknight · Freezing · About 20 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 20 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 1½ lb before trimming losses

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb yellow, sweet or red onions, weighed before trimming
  • 8 oz leeks, white and pale green parts

Directions

  1. Peel and wash onions, then dice into approximately ¼-inch pieces. Split leeks lengthwise and rinse thoroughly between layers to remove grit; slice thinly and drain very well.
  2. Spread onions and leeks separately on lined freezer trays in thin layers. Freeze until firm.
  3. Combine in cooking portions and immediately seal in labeled freezer bags with excess air removed. Keep frozen.
  4. Use directly from frozen in soups, braises or sautéed bases, cooking until tender. Do not expect fresh-onion or leek texture.

Notes & serving ideas

NCHFP supports diced onion freezing without blanching; UNH includes cut leeks among no-blanch exceptions. MSU advises that frozen leeks can become mushy and bitter. Try a small batch before committing a large harvest. Green onions may be frozen separately, but are not weighed in this batch.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb yellow onions. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
