Greek Lemon-Herb Freezer Starter

Mediterranean · Freezing · About 20 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 20 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 6–8 portions of 2 tbsp

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup fresh Meyer lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp finely grated Meyer lemon zest
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh Greek oregano
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh thyme
  • 1 tsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced

Directions

  1. Wash citrus before zesting and rinse the herbs. Combine juice, zest, herbs and garlic in a clean bowl. This starter contains no raw meat or oil.
  2. Measure into 2-tbsp portions in a clean small silicone tray and freeze immediately.
  3. Transfer solid portions to a labeled freezer bag. Keep frozen.
  4. For a marinade, thaw 2 portions (¼ cup) in the refrigerator, then whisk with 2 tbsp olive oil and season for about 1 lb meat or vegetables. Marinate meat only in the refrigerator; discard used marinade that touched raw meat.

Notes & serving ideas

A culinary freezer blend, not a shelf-stable preserve. Add oil only when using a thawed portion. Yield is approximate because herbs change volume. The planner does not estimate whole-lemon weights from juice volume.

No primary-crop weight estimate is provided; measure actual ingredients and finished portions.
