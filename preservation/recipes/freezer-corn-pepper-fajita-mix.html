Freezer Corn and Pepper Skillet Mix

Mexican · Freezing · About 45 min active prep; total estimate 4 hr 45 min including freezing. Follow the doneness endpoint rather than the clock.

Yield: About 4 skillet portions

Storage: Keep frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below. Thaw in the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below, or use directly in cooking as directed. Use thawed portions within 3 days as a conservative household practice. This recipe is not shelf-stable.

Ingredients

  • 4 ears fresh sweet corn, husked and washed
  • 1 lb bell peppers or sweet Italian peppers, washed
  • Ice and cold water, for cooling

Directions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a vigorous boil and prepare an ice-water bath. Work in small batches so the water returns to a boil promptly.
  2. Blanch whole corn ears for 4 minutes after boiling resumes. Cool completely in ice water, drain, then cut kernels from the cobs to about two-thirds of their depth. This timing is for cutting kernels off, not serving frozen corn on the cob.
  3. Remove pepper stems, seeds and membranes; cut into ½-inch strips. Blanch strips for 2 minutes after boiling resumes, then cool promptly in fresh ice water and drain.
  4. Spread cooled, well-drained corn and peppers on separate lined trays. Freeze until firm, then combine in meal portions and seal in labeled freezer bags.
  5. Cook directly from frozen in a hot skillet with oil and seasoning added at cooking time.

Notes & serving ideas

The reference batch reserves only the 1 lb peppers; corn is a secondary ingredient and is not deducted by the planner. Yield depends on ear size. Do not apply these blanching times to other vegetables.

Harvest reference batch: 1 lb bell peppers. Only this primary crop is deducted; secondary ingredients and finished yields are not calculated.
