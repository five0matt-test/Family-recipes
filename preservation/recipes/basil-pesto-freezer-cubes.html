Genovese Basil Pesto Freezer Cubes

Italian · Approx. 25 minutes

Storage: Frozen. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 packed cups Genovese basil leaves, washed and thoroughly dried
  • ⅓ cup toasted pine nuts or walnuts
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus a little if needed
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, optional

Directions

  1. Pulse basil, nuts, garlic and Parmesan in a food processor until chopped.
  2. With motor running, add olive oil gradually until a spoonable paste forms; season with salt and optional lemon.
  3. Immediately portion into small freezer-safe silicone molds or containers; cover and freeze promptly.
  4. Once solid, transfer portions to labeled airtight freezer bags. Keep frozen until needed.
  5. Thaw individual portions in the refrigerator or stir directly into hot cooked pasta or sauce. Do not store pesto at room temperature.

NCHFP freezing pesto guidance

Notes & serving ideas

Use for pasta, grilled chicken, sandwiches and vegetable bowls. For best flavor use within 2–3 months. Do not can pesto or store homemade garlic-in-oil pesto unrefrigerated. If not freezing immediately, refrigerate fresh pesto and use within 3 days.
