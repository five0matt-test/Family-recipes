Greek Beef Souvlaki with Lemon Potatoes and Village Salad

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef sirloin, cut into 1¼-inch cubes
  • 1/4 cup olive oil, for beef
  • 2 lemons, juiced, for beef
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp dried Greek oregano
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, wedged
  • 1/4 cup olive oil, for potatoes
  • 1 lemon, juiced, for potatoes
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced, for potatoes
  • 1 tsp Greek oregano, for potatoes
  • 3 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 4 oz feta cheese
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil, for salad
  • 1 tsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano, for salad

Instructions

  1. FRIDAY: Cube beef; refrigerate safely. Mix beef marinade separately. Parboil potato wedges 6–8 minutes, drain, cool promptly and refrigerate. Wash salad vegetables.
  2. SUNDAY: Marinate beef with olive oil, lemon, garlic and oregano for 30–60 minutes in refrigerator; add salt before cooking. Do not leave in acidic marinade since Friday.
  3. Heat oven to 450°F. Toss potatoes with olive oil, lemon, broth, garlic and oregano. Roast 20–30 minutes until tender and browned.
  4. Thread beef on skewers and grill or sear over high heat 6–10 minutes depending on size and desired doneness; rest 5 minutes.
  5. Combine tomatoes, bell pepper and red onion; top with feta and dress with olive oil, vinegar and oregano just before serving.

Notes

Pork and chicken are more common souvlaki proteins in Greece; beef is a family adaptation. Village salad traditionally contains no lettuce.
