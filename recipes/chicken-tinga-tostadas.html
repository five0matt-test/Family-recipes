Chicken Tinga Tostadas — Bone-In Thighs

Smoky chipotle-tomato shredded chicken tostadas with fresh toppings.

Prep: 20 minCook: 65 minServings: 4 + leftovers

Ingredients

  • 4 lb bone-in chicken thighs, skin removed
  • 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (14–15 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo
  • 1 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 12 tostada shells
  • 2 avocados
  • 2 cups finely shredded green cabbage
  • 6 oz queso fresco
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 1/3 cup cilantro
  • Fermented red onions, optional

Directions

  1. Remove skin from chicken thighs and season with kosher salt.
  2. Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown thighs about 3 minutes per side in batches; transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Cook sliced onions for 8–10 minutes until softened and lightly golden. Stir in garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Blend crushed tomatoes, chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, oregano, cumin, and chicken broth until smooth.
  5. Add sauce to the pot and simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. Return thighs to the pot bone-side down. Cover and simmer gently 35–40 minutes, until tender and cooked through (at least 165°F).
  7. Remove chicken; discard bones and shred meat. Reduce sauce uncovered 10–15 minutes until it clings to the meat.
  8. Return shredded chicken to sauce and simmer 3–5 minutes. Adjust salt.
  9. Serve on tostadas with cabbage, avocado, queso fresco, crema, lime, cilantro, and optional fermented onions.

Notes

Adapted from our October 2026 meal plan for 4 lb bone-in thighs. Bone-in cooking time differs from the original boneless version. Tostada toppings are included in the ingredient list.
