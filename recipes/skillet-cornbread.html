American · Side · Serves: 8 wedges Time: 30 minutes

Skillet Cornbread

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Yellow cornmeal
  • 1 cup All-purpose flour
  • 1 Tbsp Baking powder
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 cup Milk or buttermilk
  • 1 Egg
  • 4 Tbsp, melted Butter
  • 1 Tbsp, optional Honey

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Put a 10-inch cast-iron skillet in the oven while it heats.
  2. Whisk cornmeal, flour, baking powder and salt.
  3. Whisk milk, egg, 3 Tbsp melted butter and optional honey. Stir wet into dry just until combined.
  4. Carefully remove the hot skillet and coat it with the remaining 1 Tbsp butter. Pour in batter.
  5. Bake 18-22 minutes until deeply golden and a tester comes out clean. Rest 5 minutes before cutting.
