Purple Radish-Herb Salsa

Ingredients

  • 5, finely diced Purple plum radishes
  • 1/4 medium, finely diced Red onion
  • 1/3 cup chopped Cilantro
  • from taco recipe, thinly Green onion tops sliced
  • 2 Tbsp Lime juice
  • 1/2-1, minced; optional Jalapeno
  • 1/3 tsp, then to taste Kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Combine radishes, red onion, cilantro, green onion and optional jalapeno.
  2. Add lime juice and salt. Toss and let stand 10 minutes.
  3. Taste immediately before serving and adjust lime or salt.
