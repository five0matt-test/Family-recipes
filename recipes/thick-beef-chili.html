American · Dinner · Serves: 4 generous servings Time: 55-65 minutes

Thick Beef Chili

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Ground beef
  • 2 medium, diced Yellow onions
  • 4 cloves, minced Garlic
  • 2 x 14.5-oz cans Diced tomatoes
  • 2 x 15-oz cans, Black beans drained/rinsed
  • 1 x 15-oz can, Kidney beans drained/rinsed
  • 3 Tbsp Tomato paste
  • 2 cups Beef broth
  • 2 Tbsp Chili powder
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp, then to taste Kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp if needed Neutral oil

Instructions

  1. Brown beef in a heavy pot over medium-high heat, working in batches if necessary so it develops color instead of steaming. Transfer excess fat if needed.
  2. Add onions and cook 6-8 minutes. Add garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano and tomato paste. Cook 45-60 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add tomatoes and beef broth, scraping the bottom well. Return beef and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Simmer uncovered 25 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add beans and simmer another 15-20 minutes until thick.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. If it is thinner than you want, simmer uncovered another 5-10 minutes.

Notes

This is intentionally thick and beef-forward so it feels different from a soup. Add hot sauce or cayenne individually rather than making the whole pot spicy.
