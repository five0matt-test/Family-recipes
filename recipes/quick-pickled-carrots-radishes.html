Quick-Pickled Carrots & Purple Radishes

Ingredients

  • 2 medium, thin matchsticks Carrots or coins
  • 3, thinly sliced Purple plum radishes
  • 1/2 cup Rice vinegar
  • 1/2 cup Warm water
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp Sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp Kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Pack carrots and radishes into a clean pint jar or covered container.
  2. Stir vinegar, warm water, sugar and salt until dissolved.
  3. Pour brine over vegetables, pressing them below the liquid.
  4. Rest 30 minutes before using. Refrigerate once cooled.

Notes

Best texture in the first 1-2 weeks. These are refrigerator pickles, not shelf-stable canned pickles.
