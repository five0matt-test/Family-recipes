Crispy Chicken Flautas with Salsa Verde

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 medium white onion, divided
  • 4 garlic cloves, divided
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 8 oz Oaxaca cheese, shredded (optional)
  • Neutral oil for shallow frying
  • 1 lb tomatillos, husked
  • 1–2 serrano chiles
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema
  • 4 oz queso fresco
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 4 radishes, sliced

Instructions

  1. Simmer chicken with half the onion, 3 garlic cloves, bay leaf and salt in water to cover, about 20–25 minutes, until cooked through (165°F). Shred; moisten with a little cooking liquid.
  2. Simmer tomatillos and serranos 5–7 minutes until just tender. Blend with remaining onion, garlic, cilantro, salt and a splash of water to make salsa verde.
  3. Warm corn tortillas until pliable. Roll each tightly around shredded chicken and a little Oaxaca cheese if using.
  4. Shallow-fry seam-side down in 350°F oil, turning until crisp and golden, 3–5 minutes total per batch. Drain on a rack.
  5. Serve with salsa verde, crema, queso fresco, lettuce, radishes and esquites.

Notes

Corn tortillas and frying give flautas their traditional texture. Heat level depends on the serranos.
