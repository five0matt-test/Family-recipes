Vietnamese · Lunch · Serves: 2 lunches Time: 35 minutes

Vietnamese-Style Lemongrass Chicken Rice Bowls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 1/2 cups dry Jasmine rice
  • 2 stalks, tender inner part Lemongrass finely minced
  • 2 cloves, minced Garlic
  • 2 Tbsp Fish sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Brown sugar
  • 1 Tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp Neutral oil
  • 2 Tbsp Lime juice
  • 2, sliced Cucumbers
  • 1, julienned Carrot
  • 2, thinly sliced Purple plum radishes
  • 2 cups shredded Leaf lettuce
  • 2, sliced Green onions
  • 1/3 cup Cilantro

Instructions

  1. Cook rice and cool enough for meal-prep containers.
  2. Mix lemongrass, garlic, 1 1/2 Tbsp fish sauce, brown sugar, soy sauce and oil. Coat chicken and marinate 20 minutes or up to 4 hours.
  3. Sear or grill chicken until browned and cooked through. Rest, then slice.
  4. For a quick dressing, mix remaining 1/2 Tbsp fish sauce with lime juice and 2 Tbsp water. Add a pinch of sugar if desired.
  5. Divide rice and chicken between two containers. Pack cucumber, carrot, radish, lettuce, green onion and cilantro separately or on the cool side of the container.
  6. Keep dressing separate and add after reheating the rice/chicken.

Notes

Add the quick-pickled carrots/radishes for extra acidity and crunch if desired.
