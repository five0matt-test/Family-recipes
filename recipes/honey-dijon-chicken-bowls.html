American · Lunch · Serves 2 · 50 minutes

Honey-Dijon Chicken, Roasted Potato and Green Bean Bowls

2

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 10 oz green beans, trimmed
  • 1/2 yellow onion, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 2 Tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp water

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes and onion with 1 Tbsp oil, thyme, half the salt and pepper. Roast 15 minutes.
  2. Season chicken with garlic powder, remaining salt and 1/2 Tbsp oil. Add to pan and roast 18–22 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Roast green beans separately with remaining oil for 10–12 minutes.
  4. Whisk Dijon, honey, yogurt, lemon juice and water. Keep sauce cold and separate.
  5. Divide chicken, potatoes, onions and green beans between two containers; cool promptly and refrigerate at 40°F or below.
  6. Reheat bowl until steaming hot (165°F), then add cold sauce. Use within 3–4 days or freeze a portion.

Notes

Make ahead on Sunday for two work lunches. Use insulated transport with ice packs.
