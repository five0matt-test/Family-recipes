Same-Day Caramelized Onion, Feta & Black Pepper Sourdough

Recipe photo coming soon.

10

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 450g bread flour
  • 300g water
  • 100g active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
  • 3g instant yeast
  • 8g fine salt
  • 150g yellow or sweet onion, thinly sliced
  • 10g olive oil
  • 85g crumbled feta
  • 1.5g coarsely cracked black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the thinly sliced onion in 10g olive oil over medium-low heat for 25–35 minutes until deep golden brown, stirring regularly. Cool completely before adding to dough.
  2. Mix bread flour, water, active starter, instant yeast, and salt until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest 20 minutes.
  3. Perform a set of stretch-and-folds. Rest 20 minutes. During the next fold or a gentle lamination, distribute the cooled onions, feta, and black pepper evenly without crushing the cheese.
  4. Continue bulk fermentation at about 75–80°F until the dough is airy and roughly 60–75% expanded. With instant yeast this may take another 45–90 minutes after the folds; judge the dough, not the clock.
  5. Shape gently into a boule and place seam-up in a floured banneton. Proof 40–60 minutes until puffy and slowly springing back when pressed.
  6. Preheat Dutch oven at 475°F. Turn dough onto parchment, score, and bake covered at 450°F for 20 minutes. Uncover, reduce to 425°F, and bake 20–25 minutes more until deep golden and the center is fully baked (about 205–210°F).
  7. Cool on a rack at least 45–60 minutes before slicing. Refrigerate leftovers promptly because this loaf contains cheese.

Notes

Same-day hybrid sourdough: the 3g instant yeast speeds fermentation while the active starter provides sourdough character. Times are estimates and depend on dough temperature and starter strength. Crumbled feta may disperse; add during lamination for distinct pockets. This recipe has not yet been photographed; a dedicated food photo is pending.
