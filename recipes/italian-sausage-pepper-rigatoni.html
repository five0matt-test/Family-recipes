Italian Sausage & Pepper Baked Rigatoni

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz Rigatoni
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 3 cups Homemade spaghetti sauce
  • 2, sliced Bell peppers
  • 1 medium, sliced Yellow onion
  • 3 cloves, minced Garlic
  • 1/3 cup torn, plus garnish Genovese basil
  • 8 oz, shredded Mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan
  • 1 Tbsp Olive oil
  • to taste Kosher salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Boil rigatoni in well-salted water until 2 minutes shy of al dente; drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet. Brown sausage, breaking it into bite-size chunks. Remove excess grease if needed.
  3. Add onion and peppers; cook 6-8 minutes until softened with some browned edges. Add garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Stir in spaghetti sauce and bring to a simmer. Fold in rigatoni, basil and half the Parmesan.
  5. Transfer to a baking dish. Top with mozzarella and remaining Parmesan. Bake 15-18 minutes until bubbling; broil briefly for browned cheese if desired.
  6. Rest 5 minutes and garnish with fresh basil.

Notes

Serve with the garlic-herb lettuce salad below. This is the week's only pasta meal.
