Korean · Dinner · Serves 4 · 25 min

Quick Korean Gochujang Chicken Bowls

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  • 4 cups cooked rice, refrigerated promptly after cooking
  • 12 oz broccoli florets
  • 2 medium carrots, thinly sliced
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp gochujang (adjust at table)
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 Tbsp water
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Instructions

  1. Whisk soy sauce, gochujang, honey, vinegar, sesame oil and water together.
  2. Heat a large skillet with half the neutral oil. Stir-fry broccoli and carrots 4–5 minutes, adding a splash of water to steam until crisp-tender; remove.
  3. Add remaining oil and thinly sliced chicken. Sear and stir-fry 5–7 minutes until fully cooked to 165°F.
  4. Add garlic for 30 seconds, then pour in sauce and simmer 1–2 minutes until glossy. Return vegetables to warm through.
  5. Reheat previously cooked rice until steaming hot (165°F). Divide into bowls and top with chicken, vegetables, green onions and sesame seeds.

Notes

20 minutes or less hands-on; 20–25 minutes total with pre-cooked rice. Mild: use 1 tsp gochujang; medium: 1 Tbsp; hot: add gochugaru or extra gochujang at the table. Keep cooked rice refrigerated and reheat thoroughly.
