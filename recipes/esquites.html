Esquites-Style Charred Corn Salad

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/3 cup cotija cheese
  • 1/2 lime, juiced (add more to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp chile powder or Tajín
  • 2 Tbsp cilantro
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat a wide skillet until very hot. Char corn in oil for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Whisk sour cream with 1 tsp milk or water until pourable, adding another tsp if needed. Toss warm corn with mayonnaise, thinned sour cream, cotija, juice of half a lime, chile powder, cilantro and salt. Taste and add more lime only if needed.
  3. Serve with extra lime and chile at the table.

Notes

Esquites are typically cooked corn dressed with lime, chile, mayonnaise and cheese; charring is a shareable adaptation. Sour cream replaces Mexican crema here: thin with 1–2 tsp milk or water and start with half a lime because sour cream is tangier. Add extra lime to taste.
