Garlic-Herb Lettuce Salad

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups, washed and dried Leaf lettuce
  • 1/4 cup torn Genovese basil
  • 1/4 cup shaved or grated Parmesan
  • 2 Tbsp Lemon juice
  • 3 Tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 small clove, finely grated Garlic
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Honey
  • to taste Kosher salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, Dijon, honey, salt and pepper.
  2. Toss lettuce and basil with just enough dressing to coat lightly.
  3. Finish with Parmesan and serve immediately.
