Mexican · Dinner · Serves: 4 Time: 35 minutes + marinating

Carne Asada Tacos + Charred Peppers

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Flank or skirt steak
  • 12-16 Corn tortillas
  • 2, sliced into wide strips Bell peppers
  • 1/3 cup Orange juice
  • 1/4 cup Lime juice
  • 2 Tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 cloves, minced Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 2 tsp Kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 Green onions
  • 2, sliced Avocados
  • 2 x 15-oz cans, Black beans drained/rinsed
  • 1/2 cup chopped Cilantro
  • for serving Lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Whisk orange juice, lime juice, olive oil, garlic, cumin, oregano, salt and pepper. Marinate steak 2-6 hours.
  2. Remove steak from marinade and pat the surface lightly dry. Grill or sear over high heat until well browned and cooked to preference. Rest 8-10 minutes.
  3. Char the bell pepper strips and whole green onions in the same pan or on the grill until tender-crisp with dark edges.
  4. Warm black beans with a splash of water and a pinch of cumin and salt.
  5. Warm tortillas. Slice steak thinly across the grain.
  6. Build tacos with steak, charred peppers/onions, avocado and the radish-herb salsa. Serve black beans alongside.

Notes

The salsa on the next page provides the crisp element and uses your garden radishes.
