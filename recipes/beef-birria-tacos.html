Beef Birria Tacos with Consommé

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef chuck roast, cut in 2-inch chunks
  • 4 dried guajillo chiles, seeded
  • 2 dried ancho chiles, seeded
  • 1 medium white onion, quartered
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 8 oz Oaxaca cheese, shredded
  • ½ cup diced white onion
  • ½ cup chopped cilantro
  • Pickled red onions and lime wedges, to serve

Instructions

  1. Toast dried chiles briefly without scorching, then soak in hot water 15 minutes until soft.
  2. Blend softened chiles with onion, garlic, tomatoes, vinegar, oregano, cumin, cloves and 1 cup broth until smooth.
  3. Place beef, sauce, remaining broth, bay leaf and salt in Instant Pot. Pressure cook on high 55 minutes; allow 15–20 minutes natural release. Alternatively slow cook on low 8–9 hours until shreddable.
  4. Remove bay leaf, shred beef, and skim excess fat from braising liquid. Season consommé with salt and lime to taste.
  5. Dip corn tortillas lightly into the surface fat of the broth, lay in hot skillet, add cheese and beef, fold and crisp 2–3 minutes per side. Serve with consommé, onion, cilantro, pickled onions and lime.

Notes

Active prep about 25–30 minutes plus cooking and taco assembly. Allow roughly 2–3 hours total for Instant Pot including chile soaking, pressure buildup, release and finishing; slow cooker 8–9 hours. For a mild family base, remove chile seeds; offer hot salsa separately.
