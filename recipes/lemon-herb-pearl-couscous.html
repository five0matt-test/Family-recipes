Mediterranean Lemon-Herb Pearl Couscous

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups dry pearl couscous
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill (optional)
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp crumbled feta (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add pearl couscous and toast for 2–3 minutes until lightly golden.
  2. Add garlic and oregano; cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add chicken broth, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 10–12 minutes or until tender.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in lemon zest, lemon juice, parsley, and dill.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Finish with feta if desired.

Notes

Dill and feta are optional. Serve with Greek Chicken Souvlaki. The complete meal recipe already includes this side; select the complete meal only when shopping for both to avoid double-counting.
