Thai · Dinner · Serves 4 · 40 minutes

Thai Panang Beef Curry (Phanaeng Nuea)

4

Ingredient amounts are estimates when scaled. Original recipe and AnyList import remain unchanged.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ lb sirloin, thinly sliced across the grain
  • 1 can (13.5–14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 3–4 Tbsp Panang curry paste
  • 3 makrut lime leaves, finely shredded, divided
  • 1½ Tbsp fish sauce, to taste
  • 1–2 tsp palm sugar
  • 1/4 cup Thai basil leaves, optional
  • 2 cups dry jasmine rice
  • 12 oz green beans
  • 2 tsp neutral oil
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced, optional garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook jasmine rice according to package directions.
  2. Scoop thick coconut cream from top of can into a pan. Simmer until glossy and beginning to separate; reduce until thick if stabilized.
  3. Fry Panang curry paste in coconut cream 2–3 minutes until aromatic.
  4. Gradually add remaining coconut milk, two lime leaves, fish sauce and palm sugar. Simmer until sauce is thick and concentrated.
  5. Add sliced beef and gently simmer 2–5 minutes, just until cooked through. Adjust salty-sweet balance and fold in optional Thai basil.
  6. Stir-fry green beans separately in hot oil, adding sliced garlic for the last minute.
  7. Serve with rice, shredded remaining lime leaf and optional bell pepper garnish. Keep green beans on the side.

Notes

Traditional Panang is rich and relatively thick, not soupy. Makrut lime leaves are central; curry pastes may contain shrimp paste.
