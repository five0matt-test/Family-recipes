How pantry comparisons work

Choose recipes, set servings or whole preservation batches, and check what you need against the shared pantry. Exact names and compatible units are calculated; substitutions, packages and uncertain amounts need your review.

Use the shared household list to sync recipes, extra items and checkmarks across devices, or keep a separate browser-only list. This list does not deduct or reserve pantry items. Edited amounts and “I have enough” choices reset when the recipes or pantry inventory change. Purchase checkmarks reset when the required amount changes.
